01 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A specialist in high-tolerance flooring

CBS is a leading specialist in high-tolerance laser screed flooring, recognised for speed, efficiency and quality. With a proven track record in large-scale concrete pours of up to 6,000 m² per day, we consistently deliver projects on time and within budget.

Our expertise covers the full project cycle from design through to installation, providing clients with end-to-end solutions and world-class workmanship — bringing global standards, including projects across the UK, to the Middle East market.

07 — CONSTRUCTION METHODOLOGY

A disciplined, 14-step pour process

From engineering and design through to final sealing, every floor follows the same tightly controlled sequence — ensuring consistent tolerance and finish across the largest projects.

1. Engineering & design 2. Sub-base compaction 3. Armoured joints 4–6. Steel & formwork 7. Concrete pouring 8. Boom laser screed 9–11. Floating & finishing 12. Sealer application 13–14. Curing & survey

09 — SFRC FLOORING DESIGN

Steel fibre reinforced concrete

Steel fibres are distributed evenly throughout the concrete, forming a homogeneous reinforcing network across the full slab — increasing post-crack strength and ductility compared with traditional rebar or mesh.
